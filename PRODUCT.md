## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: hiring managers and recruiters for senior technical product and business development roles in crypto, fintech and AI. They are screening many candidates quickly and need to judge seniority, scope and fit in under a minute.
Secondary: companies looking to hire William or Ekinoxis for product, BD or crypto consulting.

## Product Purpose
William Martinez Bolaños's public portfolio and CV at 0xwomb.xyz. It proves he is a senior technical product manager who ships, and turns a qualified visitor into an email conversation. Success: a hiring manager or client emails him.

Primary action: **email** (hi@0xwomb.xyz; a job-specific william@0xwomb.xyz is planned). Secondary: download the PDF CV.

## Positioning
A technical product manager with high agency and a problem-solving orientation, backed by things another PM cannot truthfully claim:
- **Builder-founder:** founded ETH Cali, Ekinoxis and Convexo.
- **Bridge to institutions:** crypto depth plus institutional negotiation, treasury, custody, OTC and compliance work.
- **Ethereum community leader:** ETH Cali steward, DEVCON volunteer lead, Ethereum Support Program grantee.
- **LATAM and global:** based in Cali, Colombia, with US entities, European degrees and three languages.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, a job application, an email signature or a conference contact, often on a phone. Content is edited by William in the admin (`/admin/site`) and stored in Supabase; the page and the PDF CV render from the same data.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, Supabase, react-pdf, deployed on Vercel from `main`.
- All portfolio content is data driven (profile, experiences, projects, education, certificates, volunteering, achievements, skills, languages, contact). Do not hardcode content that the admin owns.
- Logos: background-removed versions in Supabase Storage `portfolio-assets/v2/`.
- The repo is public: no private data in code.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: William Martinez Bolaños. Site brand: **0xwomb**. Related brands: Ekinoxis (brand kit in `public/brand-kit/`), Convexo, ETH Cali.
- **Voice:** executive and direct. Short sentences, no filler, no long paragraphs. **Do not use dashes** (hyphens or em dashes) as punctuation in copy.
- New portrait (Brooklyn Bridge, NYC skyline): `portfolio-assets/v2/portrait-nyc.jpg` (768×1024; a higher resolution original is pending).

## Evidence on Hand
Real roles, projects, degrees, certificates, volunteering and awards in the database. Concrete outcomes already written in role bullets (e.g. 35 students, $4K DeFi volume in 8 weeks; +$20,000 in tokens secured at Giveth). No testimonials, client logos for services, or case studies exist: do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Seniority in seconds: a hiring manager should grasp role, scope and proof above the fold.
2. Proof over adjectives: outcomes, founded projects and named organisations carry the claims.
3. One clear next step: email, everywhere it matters; the CV is the backup.
4. Executive brevity: every line earns its place.
